## bookshop-front.avif

透明背景: false

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: front-on architectural elevation texture for a three-dimensional game building, NOT a perspective background. Image 1 is the exact art-style and materials reference. Match its richly detailed painted anime background, creamy old limestone, dark charcoal wooden shop frames, warm illuminated shop interiors full of merchandise, richly textured climbing ivy and flowers, subtle hand-painted stone wear, and delicate golden daylight from upper left. Create ONE rectangular building facade, width 7.2 metres, height 10.8 metres, tightly filling the entire portrait image edge to edge. TRUE ORTHOGRAPHIC FRONT VIEW, parallel horizontal and vertical lines, NO vanishing point or perspective, NO side walls visible. Ground floor occupies lower 32% of image height, two residential upper floors occupy the upper 68%, each with two large French windows with stone surrounds, shutters and narrow flower balconies. All masonry and window surfaces must be opaque, no holes or transparent windows. Building touches exact left/right/bottom/top borders; do not add sky, pavement, trees standing in front, benches, signboards, flower boxes on the ground, or people. The roof will be modeled separately, so the top border ends at a straight cornice. NO canvas awning, because a projecting fabric awning will be modeled separately. Keep both the storefront and upper floors highly detailed; not a low-poly render, not generic flat colors, not a drawing with white paper around it. Subject: the original bookshop 'Librairie des Rêves': a dark timber glazed bookshop entrance in the centre, large windows showing packed bookshelves, book covers, postcards and softly glowing brass lamps on either side. A dark rectangular carved gold serif shop-name fascia directly above the glazing reads 'Librairie des Rêves'. Pale limestone pilasters and dense ivy climbing up both sides. Original burgundy/red and honey-gold identity through books, flowers and the name fascia. Portrait, exact building elevation.
